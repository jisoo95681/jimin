# Corporate Issuers: key notes

## Dividends and buybacks
- Target payout adjustment: ΔD = (expected EPS × target payout – previous D) × (1 / adjustment years). Multi-year = chain the years.
- Debt-funded buyback: EPS after = (earnings – after-tax interest) / shares left. EPS rises if E/P > after-tax cost of debt.
- Higher payout: ↓ manager–shareholder conflict (less cash for negative-NPV projects), ↑ shareholder–bondholder conflict.

## Governance and ESG
- Dispersed ownership + dispersed voting → principal-agent conflict. **Concentrated voting power** (dual-class shares, even with dispersed ownership) → **principal-principal** conflict (controllers vs minority). A shareholder-CEO is aligned, not a conflict.
- Red flag: CEO also chairs the board (CEO duality). Board needs a minimum of independent directors, not all. **Remuneration, audit and nomination committees must be independent** (CEO on remuneration committee = problem); governance committee needs less.
- Activist shareholder: minority stake, aims to influence the company (not majority ownership, not the fund's own value at the company's expense).
- ESG integration: material quantitative + qualitative factors → history, forecasts and **peers** → adjust financials/valuation. Equity: adjust cost of equity or cash flows; debt: yield spread widens.
- Governance information is regulated and consistent; ESG disclosure is voluntary and uneven.

## Cost of equity
- Historical ERP biases: survivorship (index backfilled with surviving firms) → up; a bad period that won't recur → down; ERP over a short-term rate on an upward curve → up. Grinold–Kroner: E(R) = dividend yield + ΔP/E + inflation + real growth; ERP = E(R) – **long-term** rf.
- r = D1 / P0 + g (D1 = next-year EPS × payout; payout constant → dividend growth = earnings growth). Build-up: risk-free + equity risk premium + size + specific-company premium.
- Shadow banking = unregulated non-bank lending. Common law → stronger investor protection → lower risk premium than civil law.

## Your traps
- Stopping at the Year 1 increase; forgetting the dividend yield in r = D1/P0 + g; mixing up which conflict (principal-agent vs principal-principal) a structure creates.
