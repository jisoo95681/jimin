# Financial Statement Analysis: key notes

## Multinational operations
| Item | Current rate | Temporal |
|---|---|---|
| Monetary assets/liabilities | current | current |
| Inventory, PP&E | current | historical |
| Revenue, SG&A | average | average |
| **Cost of sales, depreciation** | **average** | **historical** |
| Translation gain/loss | equity (CTA) | net income |
- Functional currency = local → current rate; = parent's → temporal.
- Highly inflationary: US GAAP → temporal; IFRS → restate (IAS 29), then current rate for everything.
- Transaction gain on a receivable = amount × (year-end rate – transaction-date rate), through income.

## Employee compensation
- Pension expense (US GAAP, no amortization) = service cost + interest cost – **expected** return.
- Share-based pay: expense at grant-date value; tax deduction at settlement value. Settlement > grant → excess tax benefit → **tax expense ↓** (US GAAP); IFRS → to equity.

## Intercorporate / quality
- Control → acquisition method. Full goodwill (US GAAP) = FV of whole entity – FV identifiable net assets. Partial = price paid – share × FV net assets.
- Cash-flow accruals ratio = [NI – (CFO + CFI)] / average NOA.

## Your traps
- Cost of sales at the historical rate under the current rate method.
