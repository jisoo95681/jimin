# Alternative Investments: key notes

## Commodities
- Total return = price + roll + collateral (collateral rate × months / 12).
- Roll = (near – new far) / near. Backwardation → positive roll for a long; contango → negative.
- Basis = spot – futures; calendar spread = near – far; backwardation = spot above futures.
- Curve theories: storage (ample stocks, storage costs → contango) · insurance / hedging pressure (producers net short → backwardation, consumers net long → contango). Balanced hedgers rule out hedging pressure.

## Hedge funds
- Stock-for-stock merger arbitrage: long target, short acquirer at the exchange ratio.
- Event-driven keeps equity beta and left-tail risk (deals break in stress). Hedge a failed deal: buy calls on the short acquirer, puts on the long target.
- Cross-sectional momentum = rank within one class, long best / short worst, net zero. Time-series = each asset on its own trend, can be net long or short.
- Crisis protection: long volatility (OTM VIX calls). Selling volatility earns the premium but loses in crises. Dedicated short: negative correlation but more volatile.
- Off-the-run vs on-the-run Treasuries = carry trade (buy off-the-run, short on-the-run); liquidity risk is the main one.
- Combined portfolio: SD cap = SD × √(variance limit); with large negative events expected use Sortino, not Sharpe.
- Fund of funds vs multi-strategy: FoF has less leverage, two layers of fees, slower reaction, less transparency.
