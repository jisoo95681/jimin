# Equity: key notes

## Multiples and momentum
- Rising earnings → forward P/E < trailing P/E.
- Normalized EPS (average ROE method) = average ROE × **current** BVPS (not average EPS, not average BVPS).
- Relative strength = (stock / index)_end ÷ (stock / index)_start, prices only, a ratio.
- EV/Sales: diverse capital structures. P/B is weak when internally built intangibles (brands) matter.
- Negative earnings + valuable resources → asset-based valuation.

## Residual income
- RI = NI – r × B, or NOPAT – (after-tax debt charge + equity charge).
- Single stage: V0 = B0 + (ROE – r) × B0 / (r – g) → g = r – (ROE – r) × B0 / (V0 – B0).
- Finite horizon: V0 = B0 + Σ PV(RI) + (P_T – B_T) / (1 + r)^T, discounted at the cost of equity.
- Persistence ω: low ← extreme ROE, big special items, high accruals; high ← low payout, high industry persistence. TV = RI_T / (1 + r – ω).

## Free cash flow
- FCFF = NI + Dep + Int(1 – t) – FCInv – WCInv = CFO + Int(1 – t) – FCInv.
- Dividends and buybacks don't change FCFF/FCFE; new debt changes FCFE.
- Two-stage: terminal value at the end of the high-growth period is discounted N years (not N + 1). FCFE at cost of equity, FCFF at WACC.

## Your traps
- Average EPS instead of average ROE; a difference instead of a ratio; low ROE ≠ low persistence.
