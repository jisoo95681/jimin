# Equity: key notes

## Multiples and momentum
- Rising earnings → forward P/E < trailing P/E.
- Normalized EPS (average ROE method) = average ROE × **current** BVPS (not average EPS, not average BVPS).
- Relative strength = (stock / index)_end ÷ (stock / index)_start, prices only, a ratio.
- Trailing P/E: adjust EPS for **non-recurring items only** (add back one-offs; keep costs that recur, e.g. acquisition charges). Higher growth → higher P/E; higher risk → **lower** P/E.
- Weighted harmonic mean P/E = 1 / Σ(w × 1/PE): mitigates large outliers, can aggravate small ones. Buy signal: justified P/E > actual P/E of the **same type** (trailing vs trailing).
- EV/Sales: diverse capital structures. P/B is weak when internally built intangibles (brands) matter.
- Negative earnings + valuable resources → asset-based valuation.

## Residual income
- RI = NI – r × B, or NOPAT – (after-tax debt charge + equity charge).
- Single stage: V0 = B0 + (ROE – r) × B0 / (r – g) → g = r – (ROE – r) × B0 / (V0 – B0).
- Finite horizon: V0 = B0 + Σ PV(RI) + (P_T – B_T) / (1 + r)^T, discounted at the cost of equity.
- RI = NI – r × **beginning** book value (dividends play no part). Justified P/B = (forecast ROE – g) / (r – g).
- Clean surplus violations: AFS unrealized gains/losses and translation adjustments of self-sustaining subsidiaries (OCI); ordinary FX transactions are fine. RI: adjust income (not book value) for non-recurring items; adjust book value for off-balance-sheet items; interest expense may misstate the cost of debt.
- Persistence ω: low ← extreme ROE, big special items, high accruals; high ← low payout, high industry persistence. TV = RI_T / (1 + r – ω).

## Free cash flow
- Model choice: non-dividend payer with changing leverage or a control view → FCFF; stable leverage → FCFE; RI when cash flows are negative or unreliable and the accounting is clean (OCI items distort income).
- Sustainable g = retention × ROE, ROE = ROA × equity multiplier. Overvalued → price grows slower than dividends.
- FCFF = NI + Dep + Int(1 – t) – FCInv – WCInv = CFO + Int(1 – t) – FCInv.
- Dividends and buybacks don't change FCFF/FCFE; new debt changes FCFE.
- FCFE = FCFF – Int(1 – t) + net borrowing (include notes payable). Equity value from FCFF = firm value + non-operating assets – debt – preferred; compare it with the market value of **equity**. Inventory run-downs are not long-term growth.
- Two-stage: terminal value at the end of the high-growth period is discounted N years (not N + 1). FCFE at cost of equity, FCFF at WACC.

## Your traps
- Average EPS instead of average ROE; a difference instead of a ratio; low ROE ≠ low persistence; ending instead of beginning equity in RI; averaging P/Es instead of E/P for the harmonic mean; keeping the 'core EPS' that drops recurring costs.
